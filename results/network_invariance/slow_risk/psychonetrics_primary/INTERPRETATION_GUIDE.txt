Comparison: Low versus High Slow Risk

PRIMARY PARAMETERIZATION
beta is constrained equal across groups in every model.
This aligns the psychonetrics comparison with the manuscript's direct
J/h formulation, in which there is no separate group-specific beta.

NESTED TESTS
1. free_J_free_h versus equal_J_free_h:
   tests whether equality of pairwise interactions worsens fit.
2. free_J_free_h versus free_J_equal_h:
   tests whether equality of activation parameters worsens fit.
3. equal_J_free_h versus equal_J_equal_h:
   tests whether activation differences are needed when J is shared.
4. free_J_equal_h versus equal_J_equal_h:
   tests whether interaction differences are needed when h is shared.

NON-NESTED COMPARISON
equal_J_free_h and free_J_equal_h are not nested in one another.
Compare them using AIC/BIC, not a likelihood-ratio test.

INTERPRETATION CAUTION
With this large sample, a very small discrepancy may yield a small p-value.
Inspect AIC/BIC, parameter magnitudes, and substantive changes alongside
the likelihood-ratio tests. Do not equate non-significance with proof of
exact equality or significance with a substantively important difference.

BASELINE PROBABILITIES
The exported baseline probabilities are P(X_i = 1) when all other symptoms
are set to zero under the fitted model. They are not observed prevalences.
